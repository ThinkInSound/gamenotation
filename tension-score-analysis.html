Tension Score Analysis

What the arena game’s measurements look like taken all together. The data is six seeded 10-minute bot sessions, three with checkpoints and three with permadeath, sampled ten times a second. Because a bot played, this shows how the game and the model behave, not how anyone felt. The live game is Tension Score.

Structure: a composition the player can learn

With structured composition, motifs follow a repeating phrase form that grows as motifs are unlocked: AAAA, AABA (song form), ABAC, then ABACABAD (rondo). The last slot of each phrase layers two motifs like a cadence, and once a form has been heard twice, each slot has a 12% chance of being swapped for an unexpected motif: a broken pattern, marked ! in the piano rolls above. The expectation model remembers the last three motifs, so it can learn these forms. The comparison runs the same game with motifs in random order (three checkpoint sessions, seeds 31 to 33).

Our formula against established measures

Excerpt

Ely-style suspense is stakes times the spread of the next update to the odds of avoiding a hit. Surprise is stakes times how far those odds moved in the last second. The Left 4 Dead-style intensity jumps on each hit, builds while enemies are close and decays over time. The Leverage Index is Ely suspense divided by its average, so it has the same shape and is not drawn separately.

What this does and does not show

It shows the model working as designed

Stakes, threat and uncertainty behave as the theory predicts. The death rule reshapes stakes, gates resolve tension, and uncertainty sees danger before damage lands.

It does not show what anyone felt

The player is a bot, so whether any of these curves matches felt tension is still open. To test it, play Tension Score while rating your own tension and see which curve tracks you best.

Motif effects are confounded in bot play

Later motif appearances come in harder zones with more layering, and the bot never learns. So the bot data can show how motifs are scheduled and how layering raises tension, but not whether familiarity lowers it. That needs a person's ratings, compared within the same zone.

The weights are first guesses

Threat's 0.4/0.6 split, the stakes scale, the 3-second futures and the intensity rates were chosen once and never tuned. Uncertainty from 24 futures also carries sampling noise of about ±0.1 in the odds, which inflates surprise.
